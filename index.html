sample_bug_report (example format)

Title: Trading an item deducts coins but the recipient never gets the item

Severity: Critical

Steps: 1) Join a server with a second player. 2) Open a trade and add one item. 3) Both accept. 4) Compare inventories and coin balances.

Expected: The item moves to the recipient.

Actual: Coins are deducted, the item is not delivered, no error is shown.

Attached: screenshots before and after, short video of the trade.
